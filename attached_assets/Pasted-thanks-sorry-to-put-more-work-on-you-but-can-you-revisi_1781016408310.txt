What the market is doing right now
Home equity: Banks and fintechs are pushing HELOCs and equity products; home‑equity borrowing for investment is explicitly on regulators’ radar because people are being pushed into risky investments (including crypto).

DeFi and tokenization: DeFi lending has matured into “boring” infra – ~$75–80B TVL, real‑world‑asset driven, more about stable yield and good risk pricing than crazy APYs.

Real estate + crypto: Real‑estate tokenization is becoming a viable way to raise capital and fractionalize property, but it is very much a securities story with SPVs, exemptions, and compliance built‑in – not a pure DeFi free‑for‑all.

So we have: lots of home equity sitting idle, better HELOC rails, more mature DeFi lending, and early RWA/tokenization – but nobody is giving a normal person a safe, non‑custodial cockpit that ties those together in a tax‑aware, leverage‑aware, low‑touch way.

Where CryptoOwnBank can sit
CryptoOwnBank’s safe lane is: “What do I own, where is it, what’s the risk, and how do I control it myself?”

That maps perfectly onto a “Borrow‑Flow” cockpit for people who:

Own homes (HELOC‑eligible).

Hold crypto and/or stablecoins.

Want to live a buy‑borrow‑die life without constant micromanagement.

Your opportunity is not “be the lender” or “run a HELOC protocol.” It’s to:

Help them see: home equity, existing loans, crypto collateral, stablecoin balances, DeFi positions, all in one non‑custodial view.

Help them design: rules/flows like “max X% LTV on the house”, “keep Y months of payments in stables”, “borrow only from protocols with specific risk profiles”, etc.

Help them automate: send alerts, rebalance triggers, and user‑signed transactions so the system behaves the way they’d want while they sleep, without you ever making eligibility or credit decisions.

Think: “non‑custodial autopilot for leveraged but sane households.”

A concrete CryptoOwnBank product concept

Map the balance sheet

Property value and HELOC/cash‑out lines + rates.

Crypto holdings and which ones they’re willing to post as collateral.

Stablecoin and DeFi positions (e.g. USDC in lending protocols, tokenized T‑bills).

Automate, but always with user control
