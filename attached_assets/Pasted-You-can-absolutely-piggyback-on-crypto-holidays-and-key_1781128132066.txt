High‑signal recurring crypto dates
These are the ones worth anchoring annual campaigns on (you don’t need all of them; pick 3–5 that align with your narrative).
•	Jan 3 – Bitcoin Genesis Block Day  
Bitcoin’s genesis block was mined on January 3, 2009, generally treated as Bitcoin’s “birthday.”[northcrypto +1]
Angle: “Genesis of your own bank” – push first‑wallet setup, custody education, and CryptoOwnBank as the beginner‑friendly on‑ramp.
•	Jan 12 – First Bitcoin transaction  
The first BTC transaction (Hal Finney receiving from Satoshi) is usually dated January 12, 2009.[withvincent +1]
Angle: “First transaction week” – reward users for doing their first on‑chain move through your flows (swap, send, or stablecoin top‑up).
•	May 22 – Bitcoin Pizza Day  
Celebrates the 10,000 BTC pizza purchase in 2010, and is one of the most culturally recognized crypto holidays with lots of memetic energy.[bitcoinfoundation +1]
Angle: “Pay with crypto, not pizza money this time” – discounts or bonus rewards for trying your payment/onboarding flows; co‑promote with food, subscriptions, or dating (synergy with PayGatedDating).
•	Early August – “Whitepaper / crisis” themed  
While the whitepaper itself is October 31, 2008, many timelines explicitly tie 2008’s financial crisis and the 2009 launch together as the crypto “response to TradFi.”[northcrypto +1]
Angle: “De‑banking awareness” – position CryptoOwnBank as the antidote to bank offboarding and fee‑drag; great for educational/email series.
•	Oct 31 – Bitcoin Whitepaper Day  
Satoshi’s whitepaper was published on October 31, 2008, and many communities use this as “Bitcoin Day.”[withvincent +1]
Angle: “Read the paper, own your keys” – run a content + signup sprint around self‑custody and long‑term wealth preservation.
•	Nov – General ‘Bitcoin month’ and conference cluster  
There is a dense cluster of major global crypto events (ETH‑style and BTC‑style conferences) around mid‑year and Q4 each year, listed on calendars like CoinMarketCal and Luma’s crypto event feeds.[coinmarketcal +1]
Angle: “Conference from your couch” – when a big event is happening, you mirror it with live streams, explainers, and timed promos for signups.
•	Chain‑specific days (if relevant to CryptoOwnBank)  
Many ecosystems celebrate their own anniversaries and upgrades (e.g., Ethereum mainnet launch in late July 2015; XRPL/XLM anniversaries). These dates are usually tracked on “crypto holidays” lists and generic crypto timelines.[coinmarketcal +2]
Angle: Hyper‑targeted campaigns per chain you support (e.g., “XRP week” focusing on remittances and low‑fee payments).
