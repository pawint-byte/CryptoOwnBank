CryptoOwnBank’s wedge
Robinhood:

Holds your assets or synthetic exposure for you.

Gives you UI, not keys.

Puts you in the “someone else tokenized this and holds the plumbing” bucket.

CryptoOwnBank:

Doesn’t custody.

Lets people aggregate, view, and plan around their XRP/XRPL‑based assets in wallets they control.

Can speak directly to the “RWAs on XRPL” narrative, but from the self‑custody / legacy plan angle.

So the play is:

“If Robinhood’s documents are your first time hearing ‘hundreds of millions in real assets on XRPL,’ cool — when you’re ready to hold real XRP and XRPL‑based assets yourself (not just tickers in an app), CryptoOwnBank is where you manage that.”

You are not competing with the Robinhood on‑ramp; you’re catching:

People who move from Robinhood → real XRP on a non‑custodial wallet.

People who acquire exposure elsewhere and then realize they want visibility across wallets and a plan for who gets it if they’re gone.
