Key Stellar Assets You Can Use

x402 on Stellar (already live)
AI agents can pay for APIs, data, services, etc. directly with Stellar assets.
There are ready MCP servers and starter templates.

Machine Payments Protocol (MPP)
Official Stellar protocol for agentic/HTTP-native payments.
Supports both one-time payments and high-frequency off-chain payment channels (very useful for agents doing many small actions).

Soroban Smart Accounts + Smart Account Kit
This is the biggest differentiator right now.
You can create accounts with programmable spending policies (max per day/week, whitelisted destinations, time windows, etc.).
Agents can be given limited delegated signing power within rules the member sets.
Projects like stellar-agent-gateway show real examples of agents discovering and paying for services while respecting on-chain policies.

Stellar AI Agent Kit (Community Fund)
Full-stack toolkit aimed at letting AI agents safely interact with Soroban contracts + MCP integration.


How This Impacts CryptoOwnBank (Honest Take)
Good news:

You can build a symmetric AI Agent experience across both chains.
Stellar actually gives you stronger on-chain guardrail options today via Soroban smart accounts. This helps with the Business Owner persona (recurring vendor payments with hard limits) or advanced yield strategies.
x402 works on both → consistent story for agent-to-service micropayments.
Everything can still follow your core doctrine: agent proposes / prepares → member reviews and signs in their wallet (Freighter on Stellar side).

The custody-safe way to use it:

Primary UX: Agent does research + drafts the transaction (or x402 payment) → member approves and signs in Freighter/Xaman. Same model as your current DCA flow.
Advanced/optional layer: Use Soroban smart accounts to set member-defined policies that the agent must obey on-chain (e.g., “This agent can only move up to $X per day from the business reserve bucket, only to approved vendors”). This gives real autonomy for high-frequency/low-value actions without breaking non-custody.

Recommendation for the site:

Treat both chains consistently in the UI/roadmap.
On the Stellar side you can go a bit further with on-chain policy guardrails earlier because the tooling is more mature.
Market it as: “Intelligent assistance on both XRPL and Stellar — your keys, your rules, your agent does the homework.”

Bottom Line
Stellar currently has more production-ready tooling for sophisticated agentic payments and programmable guardrails than XRPL (thanks to Soroban + active x402/MPP work). Ripple’s new kit is catching up fast with official support.
For CryptoOwnBank this is net positive — you get good options on both chains without compromising the “member always signs” principle.
