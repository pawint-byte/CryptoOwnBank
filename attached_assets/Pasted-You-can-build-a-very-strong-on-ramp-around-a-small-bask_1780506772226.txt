You can build a very strong on‑ramp around a small basket of stablecoins and chains, with USDC as the core. Here’s a concise ranking focused on practical use for you and your members — cost, availability, and ecosystem support.

1. USDC – primary on‑ramp and bridge
Role for CryptoOwnBank:
Your main “buy into crypto” stablecoin and default bridge asset.

Why:

Widely supported by on‑ramps and off‑ramps (Stripe, Coinbase Onramp, MoonPay, Ramp, etc.).

Strong regulatory posture; issued by Circle, backed by cash/Treasuries.

Deep DeFi and exchange support.

Best chains for USDC (in order):

Base (USDC on Base) – best default for you

EVM‑compatible, cheap fees, good integration with modern DeFi tooling.

Plays nicely with your existing EVM/Li.Fi swap stack.

Polygon (USDC.e / native USDC on Polygon)

Very low fees, wide support on DEXes and bridges.

Solana (USDC-SPL)

Extremely fast and cheap; increasingly used for payments and remittances.

Ethereum mainnet

Highest liquidity, but expensive gas; treat as an advanced chain, not default.

2. USDT – liquidity workhorse
Role:
Secondary rail for swaps where liquidity is king.

Why:

Largest stablecoin by market cap and trading volume.

Universally listed on CEXes, DEXes, and bridges.

Tradeoff:

More debate around reserves and regulatory scrutiny versus USDC.

Best chains:

Tron USDT (TRC‑20) – extremely low fees, widely used for transfers.

Ethereum USDT – highest liquidity but high fees; again, an advanced option.

Use USDT as an option for experienced users and as a routing asset in swaps, but keep USDC as your primary “member‑facing” stablecoin.

3. DAI – DeFi-native complement
Role:
Optional DeFi‑focused stablecoin for power users.

Why:

Over‑collateralized design and long DeFi history.

Chain:

Ethereum + L2 EVMs (Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, etc.) where DAI has strong on‑chain liquidity.

You don’t need DAI for your core on‑ramp, but supporting it in your EVM swap tools makes sense.

4. RLUSD – Ripple ecosystem stablecoin (what you hold)
Role:
XRPL‑native stablecoin and “house” asset inside XRP‑centric flows.

Why:

Ties neatly into the Ripple/XRPL ecosystem and can anchor XRPL‑based experiences as it grows.

Chain:

XRP Ledger (XRPL) only – that’s where it matters.

Treat RLUSD as a specialist rail for XRPL (alongside XRP), not your main global on‑ramp coin.

5. PYUSD – PayPal USD (optional)
Role:

Optional stablecoin if you ever integrate PayPal‑linked user flows.

Why:

Backed by PayPal + Paxos; used in PayPal’s own ecosystem.

Chains:

Ethereum and supported EVM networks.

Nice‑to‑have, not essential for your basic member flow.
