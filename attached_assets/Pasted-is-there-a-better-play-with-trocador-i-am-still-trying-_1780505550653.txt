Yes — there is a better play, and it’s to stop looking for one universal “coin of everything” and instead use the best bridge asset by zone. XRP is important, but it is not the best universal source asset for today’s swap infrastructure. In practice, USDC is the strongest default bridge asset for most flows because it is broadly supported across onramps, wallets, chains, and cross-chain tools, while XRP is more concentrated around XRPL-specific use cases.

Best source asset
If your goal is “one thing most users can buy, then swap from,” the best answer is usually:

USDC first

ETH second

BTC third for certain native swap rails

XRP only for XRPL-native flows

Why USDC wins:

It is built to be a neutral, liquid bridge asset and is widely used across chains.

It appears in many swap and exchange routes, including long-tail conversions like USDC→XMR.

Stablecoins are commonly used as the transport asset in cross-chain infrastructure, not XRP.

Why XRP is weaker as the universal hub:

XRP is important, but the broader market increasingly uses stablecoins as the transfer layer, even for cross-border use cases where XRP used to be the obvious candidate.

Outside XRPL, it is not the default routing asset for EVM bridges, stablecoin rails, or many swap aggregators.

Better play with Trocador
The better use of Trocador is not “make XRP the center of everything.” It is:

use Trocador as your long-tail escape hatch,

especially for assets and pairs that DeFi bridges and mainstream onramps don’t cover cleanly,

with USDC/ETH/BTC as the likely source assets users bring into it.

That means:

Li.Fi / DEX / bridge stack handles the mainstream EVM and cross-chain world.

XRPL DEX handles XRP-native tokens.

Stellar DEX handles XLM-native tokens.

Trocador handles weird pairs, privacy coins, and “I have asset A but need obscure asset B” situations.

That is a much stronger architecture than trying to make XRP the universal starting asset.

Best practical solution
The closest thing to a “one asset all can swap from” is:

Default buy asset = USDC

If user already has another major asset:

accept ETH, BTC, USDC, XRP, XLM as source assets

Route by asset family:

USDC / ETH → EVM + cross-chain routers first

XRP → XRPL DEX first

XLM → Stellar DEX first

BTC / XMR / outliers → Trocador or specialized swap rails

That gets you much closer to “usable for most people, with only a few exemptions.”

Recommendation
So the better play is:

Do not center the whole product on XRP as the bridge coin.

Center it on USDC as the default entry and universal bridge asset.

Treat Trocador as your coverage extender, not your front door.

Keep XRP/XLM flows as native specialist lanes, because that’s where they shine.

If you want one sentence to guide the rebuild, it’s this:

“Buy USDC if you need an entry asset; otherwise bring whatever you already own, and CryptoOwnBank routes you to the best swap lane for that asset.”

The next best step is to define a source-asset hierarchy for the app:

Tier 1: USDC

Tier 2: ETH, BTC

Tier 3: XRP, XLM

Tier 4: everything else via Trocador or custom routes

That would give you the cleanest “mostly universal” solution without overpromising total coverage.
