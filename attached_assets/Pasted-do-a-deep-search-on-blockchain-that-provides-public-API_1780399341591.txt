What to build
The highest-value portfolio expansion is a Member Intelligence Layer: connect wallets, show balances and historical activity across more chains, surface DeFi positions, add wallet-level risk flags, and translate it all into plain-language guidance. That turns CryptoOwnBank into a stronger self-custody command center without becoming an exchange, bank, or transfer intermediary.

Three concrete features stand out:

Portfolio Health Dashboard: unified balances, transaction history, concentration, idle assets, protocol exposure, and wallet-by-wallet net worth using CoinStats or Tatum as the broad data layer.

Safety Intelligence Suite: address screening, sanctioned/high-risk exposure warnings, suspicious counterparty flags, and “before you send/connect” alerts using TRM-style screening.

DeFi Visibility, not control: show open positions, rewards, LPs, and protocol exposure through APIs like Moralis or CoinStats, while keeping the actual action on the user’s wallet/provider side.

Regulatory guardrails
To stay true to your model, keep the product in these lanes: read-only ingestion, user-authorized wallet views, educational prompts, risk scoring, tax/reporting exports, and outbound links or wallet handoff flows where the member acts directly. Avoid flows where CryptoOwnBank receives assets, batches user funds, chooses protocols for users, auto-routes deposits, or presents yields in a way that implies you are the operator or promoter of the investment itself.

A simple internal rule would help: if an integration makes CryptoOwnBank the decision-maker, holder, transmitter, or matcher of value, reject it; if it makes CryptoOwnBank the viewer, explainer, organizer, or screener, it is much more likely to fit your guardrails. Example: “show this wallet has exposure to protocol X and here are the risks” is safer than “deposit here through us for yield.”

Recommended stack
My practical recommendation is a 3-layer stack:

Primary data layer: CoinStats first for fastest breadth test, because it publicly supports wallet balances, transactions, portfolio endpoints, and market data through a public API with a free-key onboarding path.

Depth layer: Moralis for EVM/Solana-heavy enrichment where you need better token/NFT/DeFi detail.

Protection layer: TRM Labs for wallet-risk screening and alerts, especially if you want a member-facing “safety check” before interacting with addresses or protocols.

That combination gives you the most immediate member value while keeping CryptoOwnBank positioned as a non-custodial intelligence platform rather than a regulated financial intermediary.
