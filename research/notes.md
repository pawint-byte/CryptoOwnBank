## Findings Log

### XRP Ledger and Flare

- RLUSD is live on XRPL; issuer-strict verification and trust-line guidance are essential.
- FXRP and earnXRP are live on Flare, but add wrapper, agent, contract and withdrawal risk.
- XRPL TokenEscrow is live for eligible issued tokens and MPTs, enabling member-signed scheduled and conditional payments.
- XLS-65/66 remains in validator voting; education only, no live earn CTA.

### Tokenized finance

- Stablecoin payments are usable now, subject to provider and jurisdiction.
- Franklin's on-chain government money fund is available to eligible US retail after issuer onboarding; it is a security, not insured cash.
- Self-custodial lending is live, but liquidation and smart-contract risk make it a later guided workflow.
- EIP-7702 batching is live but persistent delegation is too dangerous for default use.

### Identity, security and continuity

- Continuous approval monitoring is retail-ready and should complement pre-sign verification.
- Private tax-lot evidence remains a strong member benefit; uncertainty must stay visible.
- Verifiable Credentials 2.0 is final, but acceptance is fragmented; avoid storing identity credentials.
- Member-selected guardian recovery is possible on supported smart accounts, but modules and estate treatment remain provider-specific.

## Conflicts & Open Questions

- Product announcements often call lending or DeFi deployment “staking”; CryptoOwnBank must identify the actual yield source.
- Retail eligibility for tokenized assets varies by state, issuer and wallet approval.
- Flare opportunities are live, but their risk is materially different from holding native XRP.
- Public-chain identity improves portability but can create permanent correlation.

## Gaps

- Before implementation, confirm exact wallet/signing support and current US eligibility for each selected provider.
- Obtain current contract addresses, audits, fees and withdrawal rules directly from official sources at integration time.
