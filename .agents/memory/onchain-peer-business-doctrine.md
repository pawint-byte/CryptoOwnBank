---
name: On-chain peer business doctrine
description: Product direction for lending and commerce: members should transact directly with machine-enforced rules, not be routed into traditional gatekept credit structures.
---

CryptoOwnBank's preferred destination is **on-chain peer-to-peer business**: members choose counterparties and terms, approve their own transactions, and rely on transparent protocol rules to hold collateral, release funds, collect repayment, or resolve a predefined outcome.

Institutional "on-chain" products that retain fund managers, borrower sourcing, credit underwriting, eligibility gates, or discretionary loss handling are not the target end state. They may be market intelligence or an optional external research reference, but must never be framed as the realization of the sovereignty mission.

**Why:** putting settlement on a blockchain does not remove a middleman if humans still decide who may participate, allocate capital, or intervene in the credit relationship. The founder's concern is member agency — doing business through machines with visible rules rather than gatekeepers.

**How to apply:** evaluate lending, escrow, marketplace, and payment features by asking whether the member can inspect terms, choose, sign, and exit directly. Favor transparent, overcollateralized or otherwise objectively enforceable protocols. Treat unsecured credit as a separate, trust/reputation problem; it cannot truthfully be marketed as fully permissionless simply because its records settle on-chain.
