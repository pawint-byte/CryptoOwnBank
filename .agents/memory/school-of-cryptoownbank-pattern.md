name: School of CryptoOwnBank teach pattern
description: House UX rule — every risky member action follows explain → verify-understanding → proceed; persona-agnostic; soft check, not a quiz wall.
---

Rule: every high-stakes/irreversible member moment uses ONE recognizable rhythm —
**Explain** (one plain sentence: what happens + what can't be undone) → **Verify** they
understood the single thing that matters (a small, specific check, not a reflex "I agree")
→ **Proceed**. Same shape everywhere, so the habit of thinking travels with the member
after they close the app.

**Why:** Founder's explicit call (2026-06). He refused to segment the experience by
persona ("degen vs normie vs business") — *"they all need to graduate from the school of
CryptoOwnBank so the thinking and behavior survives the day. The way you do anything is
the way you do everything."* Consistency itself is the product, not any single screen.

**How to apply:**
- The **Verify** beat fires ONLY at irreversible / high-stakes forks. Reversible actions
  stay frictionless — verifying everything trains reflex click-through on the dangerous ones.
- **Soft** comprehension check, not a hard quiz wall: a wrong answer re-explains warmly and
  re-asks; never scold, never permanently block.
- Templates already in the codebase to copy the feel: the Legacy Plan **vault-passphrase**
  step (Understand-the-Risk → Test Decrypt → save disabled until encryption actually
  succeeds) and the **Annual Review** (6-point checklist before the Attest button unlocks).
- First rollout target: **Legacy Plan**, fixing the spots where stakes are high but
  understanding is currently only *assumed* — the dead-man-switch schedule/grace period,
  the Last-Resort Fallback on/off either-or, and split-delivery M-of-N.
- Build it once as a reusable "teach + verify" block so future risky moments (first send,
  seed backup, contract approvals) drop it in and instantly match.
