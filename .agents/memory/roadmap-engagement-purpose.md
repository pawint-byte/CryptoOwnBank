---
name: Roadmap as an open invitation
description: Why the public member roadmap stays broad instead of being reduced to a narrow prioritization ballot.
---

Keep the public roadmap broad. Its primary purpose is engagement and belonging: visitors browse many possible directions, create a free account to vote, suggest an unlisted need, and return to see whether interest becomes action.

**Why:** The founder explicitly chose a wide net over a tightly curated ballot. Different people should be able to recognize their own needs in the pipeline, even when an idea is early or externally dependent.

**How to apply:** Preserve honest statuses, feasibility language, visible progress, and an easy “what are we missing?” path. Do not narrow the list merely to improve ranking quality; votes show interest and do not automatically promise construction.
