---
name: Inheritance readiness claims
description: Marketing and readiness language must distinguish confirmed people from complete recovery coverage.
---

Never claim a Legacy Plan has complete inheritance coverage merely because all beneficiaries are confirmed. Full-readiness language requires every intended wallet to have recovery instructions and every configured SLIP-39 plan to have its required shards correctly assigned and tested.

**Why:** Beneficiary confirmation proves the people can be reached; it does not prove every asset can be recovered. Conflating the two creates a dangerous false sense of safety.

**How to apply:** Public/meta/FAQ copy must describe the Legacy Plan as a planning and instruction-delivery tool. In product readiness UI, keep valid beneficiary ratios such as 3/3, but gate “fully ready” or equivalent claims on wallet and configured-shard completeness.
